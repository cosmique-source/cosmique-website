For vinyl collectors

The tool you'd build yourself if you had the time.

Discogs is a marketplace. Your spreadsheet is a spreadsheet. Roon doesn't know what a matrix number is.

Orbit is the first music server that treats vinyl as first-class data. Every pressing tracked separately. Every rip tagged with the cartridge that made it. Every piece of condition language you cared to write, preserved.

What you can record

  • Label, catalog number, country of pressing, year.
  • Matrix / runout etchings.
  • Jacket and media condition with free-text notes.
  • Provenance — purchase date, price, seller.
  • Equipment chain — turntable, cartridge, phono stage, ADC, software.
  • Discogs identifier linking back to the marketplace.

What you can do with it

  • Browse your collection by label, era, genre, pressing country.
  • Filter to “all Blue Note original mono pressings” in two taps.
  • See vinyl and digital copies of the same album side by side.
  • Mark which pressing is your favourite, and why.
  • Export your catalogue — your data, always.
